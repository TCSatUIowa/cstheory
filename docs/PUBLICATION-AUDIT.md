# Publication and news audit

Reviewed September 16, 2026. The website now contains **189 verified publication records**, spanning 1997–2026. This is an expanded, checked bibliography, **not a claim of exhaustive coverage**. Some older records remain unresolved below. Conference and journal versions are separate records when separately published; the count is not a count of distinct research projects.

## Scope and inclusion rule

The review covers the four current faculty, seven current students, and all 21 alumni currently listed in `content/people/`. Include a paper when at least one of those people has a documented University of Iowa affiliation on the paper, its publisher metadata, or a relevant institutional record. Joint affiliations count. An alumnus's subsequent paper can also qualify through a current Iowa group coauthor; it does not imply that the alumnus was still at Iowa. Papers from unrelated namesakes, or work solely at other institutions, do not qualify.

Author names and order come from publisher/Crossref records or the paper itself. Faculty pages and DBLP were used for discovery, not to infer affiliations from dates. Conference years follow proceedings citations; journal years follow the issue or final publication record. Preprints and accepted papers retain those labels. A brief announcement is identified in its title. The list also includes published workshop contributions and conference abstracts in journals where those were explicitly identified by the sources.

This is a manual, evidence-based snapshot. No live Google Scholar scraping or automatic author-name matching was added to the website. `content/publications.md` remains the only publication content file to edit. This document is a record of this audit, not a runtime dependency or a requirement for routine edits.
